# Compliance templates

These documents are **templates**. The product operator and each organization must confirm registration duties under Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (and 2021 regulations) with a qualified Ugandan lawyer. They are not legal advice.

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| [DPIA template](dpia-template.md) | Data-protection impact assessment outline |
| [Processing register](processing-register.md) | Record of processing activities |
| [External services](external-services.md) | What leaves the platform and why |
| [Sovereign mode](sovereign-mode.md) | `ALLOW_EXTERNAL_AI_CALLS=false` operation |

Public commentary from 2025 reports that the Personal Data Protection Office expects controllers, processors and collectors to register and renew annually, and to show a framework for cross-border transfers.
